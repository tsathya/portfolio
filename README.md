# Medhya — product management portfolio

A one-page PM portfolio: experience, projects, how you work, skills, and contact.

## How to run it

1. In Cursor’s left sidebar, right-click `index.html`.
2. Choose **Open with Live Server** if you have that extension, **or**
3. In Finder, double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.

## What’s in here

- `index.html` — hero, experience (newer/older), projects, approach, about, contact
- `style.css` — layout, type, light/dark theme
- `script.js` — theme toggle + expanding projects

## Make it yours

- Put your email in the contact `mailto:` link.
- Replace the sample metrics and stories with real products.
- Drop in screenshots, PRDs, or research notes inside each case body.
